# Line Free India - Customer Walkthrough Script

**Target Audience:** Customers, App Users
**Duration:** 2-3 minutes
**Language:** Hinglish
**Tone:** Guided, Helpful, Premium

## Scene 1: Introduction & App Open (0:00 - 0:20)
- **Visuals:** Beautiful splash screen of Line Free India. App opens to the Home screen showing the greeting "Good evening, Rahul" and the user's avatar.
- **VO:** "Welcome to Line Free India! India ka apna beauty aur wellness SuperApp. Chaliye dekhte hain kaise aap apna time bacha sakte hain."
- **Text On Screen:** Premium Experience Starts Here ✨

## Scene 2: Discovery & Search (0:20 - 0:45)
- **Visuals:** User scrolling through categories (✨ All, 💈 Salon, 💅 Parlour, etc.). Taps on the Search bar. Scrolls through the "Nearby places" list showing distance (e.g., 1.2 km) and Live status (🟢 Open).
- **VO:** "Home screen par aap apne aas-paas ke top salons aur spas discover kar sakte hain. Live status dekhiye—kaun open hai aur kiski kitni doori hai."
- **Text On Screen:** Find Nearby Salons 📍

## Scene 3: Salon Detail & Service Selection (0:45 - 1:15)
- **Visuals:** Taps on a salon named "Elite Universe". Shows the beautiful Salon Detail page with Aurora background. User checks the "Services" tab, selects multiple services: Hair Cut (₹250) and Beard Trim (₹150).
- **VO:** "Kisi bhi salon ki details check karein—unke services, prices, photos aur reviews. Apni pasand ki services select karein. Ek saath multiple services choose karna bahut aasan hai."
- **Text On Screen:** Transparent Pricing & Multi-select ✂️

## Scene 4: Joining the Queue (1:15 - 1:40)
- **Visuals:** User taps "Get Token". The confirmation screen appears showing "Token #24 Confirmed!" and an estimated wait time of "~28 mins".
- **VO:** "Bas ek tap se queue join karein! Aapko apna digital token number mil jayega, aur exact wait time pata chal jayega. Agar emergency hai, toh Tatkal booking bhi available hai."
- **Text On Screen:** Digital Token & Live Wait Time ⏱️

## Scene 5: Live Tracking & Smart Arrival (1:40 - 2:10)
- **Visuals:** App switches to the Live Tracking screen. A circular progress bar shows "3 people ahead". A notification slides in: "Leave in 8 minutes 🚗 to reach on time."
- **VO:** "Ghar baithe apni baari ka intezaar karein. App aapko live track karke batayega ki aapke aage kitne log hain. Aur haan, 'Smart Arrival' aapko yaad dilayega ki kab ghar se nikalna hai."
- **Text On Screen:** Smart Arrival Notification 🔔

## Scene 6: Service Complete & Reviews (2:10 - 2:30)
- **Visuals:** Token status changes to "✅ Completed". User rates the salon 5 stars and earns loyalty points. Shows the "Queue Passport" screen with visit history.
- **VO:** "Service complete hone ke baad aasan payment, aur reviews add karein. Har visit par points earn karein jo aap next time use kar sakte hain."
- **Text On Screen:** Earn Loyalty Rewards 🎁

## Scene 7: Outro (2:30 - 2:50)
- **Visuals:** Montage of different app screens (Favorites, Community Board, Wallet). Line Free India logo with download badges.
- **VO:** "Line Free India — Apna time bachao, queue se chutkara pao! Download the app now and experience the VIP treatment."
- **Text On Screen:** Apna Time Bachao! Download Now.
