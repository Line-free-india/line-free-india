# Line Free India - Business Walkthrough Script

**Target Audience:** Salon Owners, Spa Managers, Wellness Business Owners
**Duration:** 3-4 minutes
**Language:** Hinglish (Professional yet friendly)
**Tone:** Empowering, Smart, Business-focused

## Scene 1: The Chaos of Managing a Salon (0:00 - 0:30)
- **Visuals:** Split screen: Left side shows a stressed salon owner managing walk-ins, phone calls, and an angry waiting customer. Right side shows a calm owner using an iPad with Line Free India Business Dashboard.
- **VO:** "Salon ka kaam manage karna mushkil hai? Customers ka wait karna, calls attend karna aur paper register maintain karna... ab ye sab purani baat ho gayi hai."
- **Text On Screen:** Say Goodbye to Chaos! 👋

## Scene 2: Easy Setup & Smart Dashboard (0:30 - 1:00)
- **Visuals:** App shows quick setup (adding business name, services, and photos). Transitions to the "Live Hub ⚡" dashboard showing "Queue Load", "Today's Revenue", and "Live Status".
- **VO:** "Meet Line Free India Business Dashboard. Apne salon ko 2 minute mein online layein. Hamara smart Live Hub aapko aapke business ka pura control deta hai."
- **Text On Screen:** Setup in 2 Minutes ⏱️

## Scene 3: Queue & Token Management (1:00 - 1:40)
- **Visuals:** The "Live Queue" widget shows 5 people waiting. Owner taps "Next Customer" to move token #24 to "Now Fulfilling". Owner taps a QR code icon, showing "Scan to Book" for offline walk-ins.
- **VO:** "Digital tokens ke saath customers ko manage karein. Jo log online book karte hain aur jo walk-in aate hain (QR code scan karke), sab ek hi smart queue mein sync ho jaate hain. No confusion!"
- **Text On Screen:** Seamless Online + Offline Queue 🔄

## Scene 4: Staff & Break Management (1:40 - 2:10)
- **Visuals:** "Team Roster" section showing staff members on/off duty. Owner taps the "🍱 60m Break" button and the shop status updates to "On Break".
- **VO:** "Apne staff ki performance track karein aur unko customers assign karein. Busy din hai? Bas ek tap se 'Break' mode on karein taaki naye online tokens temporarily pause ho jayein."
- **Text On Screen:** Smart Staff & Break Management ☕

## Scene 5: Analytics & Revenue Growth (2:10 - 2:50)
- **Visuals:** Scrolling down to "Hourly Heatmap", "Top Performing Services", and "Revenue Pulse" (7D/30D view). Shows client mix (60% returning).
- **VO:** "Data is power! Hourly heatmap se janiye aapke peak hours kaun se hain. Konsi service sabse zyada bik rahi hai, aur aapka daily revenue kahan ja raha hai. Sab kuch real-time!"
- **Text On Screen:** Real-time Analytics & Heatmap 📊

## Scene 6: CRM & Marketing (2:50 - 3:20)
- **Visuals:** "Marketing" section showing active promo codes (e.g., 20% OFF). Showing a "Low Inventory Alert" for hair wax. 
- **VO:** "Khali time mein zyada customers chahiye? Flash sale aur promo codes create karein. Automated alerts aapko low stock items ke baare mein inform karte rahenge."
- **Text On Screen:** Grow Revenue with Smart Marketing 🚀

## Scene 7: Conclusion (3:20 - 3:50)
- **Visuals:** Happy salon owner attending to a customer. Graphics pop up: More Customers, Less Chaos, Higher Revenue. Line Free India Business logo.
- **VO:** "Zamana smart ho gaya hai, apne business ko bhi smart banaiye. More customers, less chaos, aur happy clients. Line Free India Business aaj hi join karein!"
- **Text On Screen:** Line Free India — Apne business ko smart banao! 💼
