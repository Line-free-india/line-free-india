# Line Free India - General Marketing Script

**Target Audience:** General public, Social Media (Reels/Shorts), Business Partners
**Duration:** 60-90 seconds
**Language:** Hinglish (Hindi + English mix)
**Tone:** Energetic, Relatable, Problem-Solving

## Scene 1: The Problem (0:00 - 0:10)
- **Visuals:** A person sitting in a crowded salon waiting area, looking at their watch frustrated. Text overlay: "Salon mein wait karke thak gaye?"
- **Voiceover (VO):** "Sunday ho ya tyohaar, salon mein ghanto wait karke thak gaye ho?"
- **Text On Screen:** ⏳ 2 Hours Later...

## Scene 2: The Solution (0:10 - 0:25)
- **Visuals:** The person takes out their phone, opens the **Line Free India** app. The beautiful dark-themed UI with "✨ All" and "Nearby 12 places" pops up. 
- **VO:** "Ab aur nahi! Meet Line Free India — India's #1 Beauty & Wellness SuperApp."
- **Text On Screen:** 📱 Line Free India

## Scene 3: Discovery & Live Queue (0:25 - 0:40)
- **Visuals:** Screen recording of the app. User taps on a nearby premium salon. The screen shows "🟢 LIVE NOW" and current wait time. They select "Hair Cut" and "Beard Trim".
- **VO:** "Apne aas-paas ke best salons dhoondo, live queue status check karo, aur ghar baithe queue join karo."
- **Text On Screen:** 🟢 Live Queue Tracking

## Scene 4: Smart Arrival (0:40 - 0:55)
- **Visuals:** Person is relaxing at home on the sofa watching TV. A notification pops up: "Leave in 8 minutes 🚗".
- **VO:** "App aapko smart arrival time batayega. Jab aapki baari aane wali ho, tabhi salon niklo."
- **Text On Screen:** 🚗 Leave Just In Time

## Scene 5: Instant Service & Rewards (0:55 - 1:15)
- **Visuals:** Person arrives at the salon, walks past the waiting crowd straight to the barber's chair. Smile on their face. The app screen shows "Token #24 - Now Serving" and then "Payment & Loyalty Points Earned".
- **VO:** "No waiting! Direct service pao, payment karo aur har visit pe loyalty points kamao."
- **Text On Screen:** VIP Treatment 👑 + Loyalty Points 🎁

## Scene 6: Call to Action (1:15 - 1:30)
- **Visuals:** Line Free India logo with download buttons for App Store and Google Play.
- **VO:** "Apna time bachao, queue se chutkara pao. Aaj hi Line Free India app download karo. Ab queue mein khade hone ki zaroorat nahi!"
- **Text On Screen:** Download Now! 🚀
